Safety rules can escalate, never down-rank

  1. 01

    Hazard detection

    Gas odors, spreading water, or sparking electrical signals.

  2. 02

    Forced escalation

    Safety rules immediately override priority to emergency.

  3. 03

    No down-ranking

    Software is never permitted to dismiss a danger signal.

If a message mentions a gas smell or flooding, deterministic rules force an immediate emergency alert. The automation cannot downgrade a safety risk.
