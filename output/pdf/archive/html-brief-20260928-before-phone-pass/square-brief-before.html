From report to completion

A complete process. Clear responsibility.

AI prepares information. The workflow follows policy. People authorize the work.

  1. 01

    Receive and connect

    Workflow

    Save reports and files; verify identity; link replies, duplicates and shared incidents. Acknowledge receipt.

  2. 02

    Interpret and check

    AI

    Suggest urgency and trade; note language, access, pets and missing details.

    Fixed rules, separate from AIOnly raise urgency. Safety alerts do not wait for clarification.
  3. 03

    Clarify and reassess

    AI + workflow

    Ask approved follow-up questions. Keep known details; reassess each reply or worsening condition.

  4. 04

    Prepare the work

    AI + workflow

    Draft PMS notes and vendor instructions. Check vendor trade, coverage, hours and compliance.

  5. 05

    Obtain the human decision

    Manager

    Review the original report and reason. Hold, edit, reclassify, assign, select a vendor or seek owner approval.

  6. 06

    Execute the approved action

    Workflow

    Update the PMS and contact the vendor once. Tell the resident only after the send succeeds.

  7. 07

    Coordinate progress

    Workflow + staff

    Track acceptance, quotes, agreed appointments and delays. Answer status questions without a new request.

  8. 08

    Verify and close

    Staff

    Staff check completion evidence and close the work order. The workflow sends the approved final update.

Who handles what

Your team decides

Human review at any stage

Daily operation & oversight

Prepared work. Visible control.

Your team sees what needs attention, who owns it, and what happens next.

The daily operating view

Prioritize work. See overdue requests, owner approvals and vendor delays in one ordered list.

Alert by policy. Follow on-call and quiet-hour rules; check unanswered emergencies. Age alone never creates an emergency.

See the portfolio. Daily summaries and reports show waiting work, exceptions and ownership.

Your team keeps authority

Vendor choice and dispatch. Spending. Appointments and commitments. Emergency downgrades. Verified closure.

Sensitive cases stay with people

Legal, accommodation and liability issues go to trained staff. Unverified identity reveals no property information.

AI recommends. Authorized staff decide.

Controls that keep work visible

If a tool fails

Keep the request. Apply fixed rules when AI fails. Queue retries; assign failed or no-vendor cases to staff.

If records disagree

Reject stale or duplicate approvals. Flag PMS differences instead of overwriting staff changes.

Protect access and data

Role-based access, MFA, encrypted data, managed credentials and agreed retention.

Keep the service maintainable

Monitor intake and the full path. Test backup restores and approved changes; keep a rollback route. No self-training.

What this gives the business

Less chasing and interruption
Questions, reminders and policy-based alerts handle routine preparation.

More reliable handoffs
Named owners and confirmed facts make resident updates and portfolio reporting clearer.
