System overview and business outcomes

The system converts maintenance messages from existing resident channels into prepared, visible work. It identifies what requires attention, obtains missing operational details, and presents the next decision while authorized employees retain control over dispatch, spending, sensitive cases, appointments, and closure.

How one request moves through the system

The same request remains one auditable record from the resident's first message through the final human-verified closure.

Running example
"There is water under the kitchen sink again and it is spreading toward the dishwasher. I turned the tap off but it is still coming out. My dog is in the unit and I can let someone in."
1

Receive and connect the request

Preserve the resident's exact words. Verify or resolve the resident, property, unit, conversation, and related open work. Detect repeated messages and follow-ups before creating anything new.

Visible resultOne intake record linked to the correct context
2

Interpret the issue and apply policy

AI interpretation

Structures the symptom, likely trade, urgency recommendation, missing details, access permission, pet information, and sensitive-case indicators.

Fixed policy rules

Apply safety, legal, notification, authority, and business rules separately. Rules may raise urgency but cannot lower it.

Example resultSpreading water triggers the company's water-escalation policy
3

Clarify and prepare

Ask only for missing operational details. Prepare the [Client PMS] record, internal summary, vendor work description, and eligible vendor options. Do not interrupt the manager while safe clarification remains possible.

Example resultAccess and pet details are already complete; plumbing options are prepared
4

Present the human decision

Show the original message, prepared summary, escalation reason, property context, authority requirements, and permitted actions. The authorized employee may hold, edit, reclassify, assign manually, request owner approval, or select a vendor.

Human checkpointThe manager selects the approved after-hours plumber
5

Execute and track the confirmed action

Claim the action once, write the confirmed result to [Client PMS], and contact the selected vendor through [Vendor Channel]. Update the resident only after delivery succeeds. Track response, appointment state, progress, verification, and human closure.

Example resultThe vendor receives one request; the resident receives a factual update with no invented ETA

Only three exception branches are needed at this level

Missing informationResident clarification↵ Reassess the same request
Sensitive, unreadable, or uncertainAssigned human reviewNo automated substantive action

Operating controls and client configuration

Production readiness depends on explicit authority, visible failure behavior, and configuration based on the client's actual policies and systems.

Automatic preparation versus human authority

The system may prepare automatically

  • Receive and preserve authorized maintenance reports
  • Link residents, units, conversations, and open work
  • Detect duplicates and attach follow-ups
  • Recommend urgency, trade, and missing information
  • Apply fixed escalation and business rules
  • Ask approved clarification questions
  • Prepare work-order notes and communication drafts
  • Propose eligible vendors without contacting them
  • Retry temporary technical failures within policy

Authorized people retain control

  • Whether a vendor is contacted
  • Which vendor is selected
  • Spend above the applicable authority limit
  • Accommodation, discrimination, legal, insurance, or liability matters
  • Changing an emergency classification downward
  • Appointments, completion times, reimbursements, or rent credits
  • Safety rules, vendor eligibility, and communication policy
  • Verification and final work-order closure

Failure behavior remains visible to the business

SituationSystem responseWhat remains visible
AI unavailable or unusablePreserve the request, run fixed rules, and assign human reviewOriginal message, failed step, rules result, and owner
PMS or communication channel unavailableQueue the action, retry within policy, and expose the unsynchronized statePending action, attempts, failure reason, and next owner
Vendor contact failsKeep the request open, notify the manager, and make no resident claimSelected vendor, delivery attempts, error, and next action
Duplicate or stale approvalAccept only the first valid claim and reject the repeated actionSuccessful actor plus every rejected attempt
Resident identity is uncertainReveal no property or work-order information and route to verificationUnverified conversation and assigned task
Data differs between systemsCreate a reconciliation task instead of overwriting silentlyBoth values, source systems, timestamps, and responsible owner
