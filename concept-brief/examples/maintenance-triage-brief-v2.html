What changes - and what does not

A new coordination layer, not another system to replace.

Your team keeps its property management platform, vendor relationships, and approval authority. The automation sits between incoming work and the next human decision.

What stays yours
  • Your property management systemLease, unit, work-order, and accounting records remain in the existing system of record.
  • Your vendors and commercial rulesApproved trades, service areas, rates, and not-to-exceed limits remain business decisions.
  • Your final sign-offManagers decide whether a work order is dispatched, held, or changed.
+
What gets added
  • OrchestrationA 24/7 workflow that receives, routes, and records incoming requests.
  • Structured interpretationAI extracts the issue; explicit rules protect known safety boundaries.
  • Durable state and approvalsA database keeps conversation, approval, send, and audit state consistent.
