Systems Architecture & Workflow Concept

Autonomous Maintenance Request Triage & Vendor Coordination

A non-disruptive operational overlay that turns unstructured tenant messages into organized, manager-approved vendor actions.

Prepared For: Highland Property Group
Attention: Marcus Vance, VP of Operations
Known PM Stack: AppFolio / Existing PM System
Document Ref: CB-2026-HPG
Why this concept was prepared for Highland Property Group: Based on your portfolio of scattered-site residential units, managing 24/7 tenant maintenance requests across email, portal, and text typically consumes dozens of coordinator hours each week—creating a constant tension between after-hours burnout and the risk of delayed habitability emergencies.
The Operational Friction in High-Volume Maintenance Intake
Invisible Cognitive Load

Maintenance requests arrive undifferentiated around the clock. Coordinators spend hours deciphering rambling emails, re-keying details into work orders, chasing tenants for missing access/pet details, and assessing urgency manually—while true emergencies risk sitting unnoticed until morning.

Unstructured Messages Tenants report issues with missing details, rambling descriptions, or misleading urgency flags.
The Two-Sided Risk Under-responding turns small leaks into $10k claims; over-responding burns budgets on weekend callouts.
After-Hours Coverage Gap Standard business hours cover 40 of 168 weekly hours; unmonitored nights leave operators exposed.
Target Operational Transformation Advisory-Only Architecture
Zero emergency maintenance reports sitting unread outside office hours, with 70%+ of routine triage, clarification, and dispatch preparation handled before human review.
Instant Risk Flagging

Dangerous language (gas, flooding, electrical) is caught in seconds by escalate-only safety rules.

Hands-Off Clarification

Missing access permissions, pet status, and symptoms are gathered directly from the resident first.

One-Tap Manager Approval

Staff review prepared vendor choices and approve dispatch with a single tap. Zero auto-dispatch.

01 Current Friction Manual
  • Unsorted email/portal inbox
  • Staff manually extracts trade & unit
  • Multiple emails to get pet/access info
  • Delayed emergency phone alerting
02 Automation Overlay System
  • Instant message normalization & dedupe
  • Strict JSON AI extraction of urgency & trade
  • Binding escalate-only safety rules
  • Automated resident clarification loop
  • Eligible vendor candidate matching
03 Target State Result
  • Emergencies wake on-call staff instantly
  • Prepared vendor draft ready for 1-tap send
  • Routine tickets synced clean into PMS
  • 100% human control over vendor contact

How The System Operates Inside Your Business

A visual walkthrough showing how inbound reports are captured, safely evaluated, clarified, and handed to your team for approval.

STAGE 01 Multi-Channel Intake
Resident Messages Trigger

Email, Portal Form, or SMS from tenant arrives 24/7.

Normalize & Dedupe Core

Strips signatures, computes body hash, filters 24h repeats.

Preliminary Buffer Guard

Vague greetings held until a concrete symptom is stated.

STAGE 02 Triage & Safety
AI Interpretation Cognitive

Extracts Trade, Room, Fixture, Access info, and preliminary Urgency.

Binding Safety Rules Escalate-Only

Deterministic regex checks 10 hazard types (gas, flood, fire). Can raise, never lower.

Durable Persistence Database

Allocates permanent reference (e.g. MR-0012) & audit history.

STAGE 03 Routing & Filters
Missing Info Tenant Clarification: Asks resident for missing access/pet/symptom details. Manager unalerted.
Sensitive Case Human Review Only: Legal threats, accommodation, or habitability disputes skip auto-drafts.
Complete & Safe Vendor Match: Filters active vendor roster by property & trade; prepares natural dispatch draft.
STAGE 04 Human Checkpoint
Manager Alert & Choices Approval Point

Mobile/Slack notification displays original words, AI summary, trade, and buttons for matched vendors.

One-Send Lock Idempotency

Database lock guarantees vendor is emailed at most once, even if tapped twice.

Approved Dispatch Action

Email sent to chosen contractor with job scope & property details.

STAGE 05 Sync & Status
PMS Synchronization PM System

Work order notes, assigned vendor, and timestamp synced to AppFolio/Buildium.

Factual Resident Update Confirmed Send

Tenant notified only after vendor send succeeds. Never promises unconfirmed arrival times.

Unclear / Garbled Message Never silently dropped. Stored with full raw text and assigned to coordinator review queue with reason flagged.
Vendor Email Delivery Fails System records failure, alerts manager for manual re-try, and explicitly blocks resident "help is coming" update.
Unanswered Emergency Automated 15-minute scheduled check monitors unacknowledged emergencies and escalates to secondary on-call roster.
