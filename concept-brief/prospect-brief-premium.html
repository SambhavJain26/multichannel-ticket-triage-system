How one request moves through the business

The request gets clearer at every handoff.

The system prepares the work. A manager still decides whether anything is sent to a vendor.

Resident report

“Water is spreading under the kitchen sink. The tap is off, but water is still coming out. My dog is inside. You may enter.”

1
Receive

Connect the message to the right request

Keep the resident's exact words. Match the resident, unit, conversation, and open work. Attach follow-ups instead of creating duplicates.

Request recordOriginal message + trusted context
2
Interpret

Read the issue, then apply fixed policy

AI structures the symptom, trade, urgency, access, and pet details. Separate safety rules may raise urgency, but never lower it.

Risk checkedAI recommendation + rule result
3
Clarify

Ask only for what is missing

The question returns through the resident's existing channel. Their reply joins the same request and the urgency check runs again.

Example follow-up“Is the water still flowing after the shutoff?”Reply returns to this request
4
Human checkpoint

Present one complete manager decision

Show the original message, urgency reason, property context, approval need, and eligible actions. The manager may approve, hold, edit, or assign.

ManagerAuthorizes the action

Confirmed handoff

Send once, then update the record

A single-use claim prevents duplicate dispatch. The resident is told a provider was contacted only after the vendor message succeeds.
